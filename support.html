Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Trailline creates one on your phone automatically. Back it up with Sign in with Apple or email (You → Account) to keep it if you change phones.
  • How do I add friends? Crew → Add friends: share your friend code or QR, scan theirs, or add their @username.
  • Who can see where I am? Only people you share with: friends and family see you; riders you haven't connected with see a rough area (~100 m), and only if you choose "Open to ride".
  • Report someone: open their card on Crew, or hold one of their messages, and tap Report. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • Delete my account: You → Account → Delete account.
  • Recording in my pocket: allow location "Always" in Settings → Trailline → Location.

If someone is hurt or in danger on the mountain, contact ski patrol or call 911.
